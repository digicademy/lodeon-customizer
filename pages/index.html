Download LODEON Customizer

Bring your own models to the LODEON Assistant, or adjust a model your workbench already offers. The app keeps your provider keys in local settings.

1 Download the app

Choose the latest build for your computer. Open the archive, extract its contents and launch LODEON-Customizer.

macOS

Find your chip under Apple menu → About This Mac.

Latest builds update automatically. Windows builds are unsigned. macOS builds are ad-hoc signed and have no Apple notarization.

2 Connect your workbench

The app opens a settings page in your browser. Paste your LODEON workbench address into Instance connection and choose Save.

In your original LODEON tab, choose Settings → Customize Workbench → Connect. Keep the app running.

3 Choose your model

In the Customizer, use Personal models → Add model to add your own. After saving, reconnect the LODEON tab and select the model in the Assistant.

Already using a workbench model? Choose Instance models → Edit settings to adjust it. Reset settings restores its original values.
